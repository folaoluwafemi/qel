# Qel — Brand Identity

## Brand Essence

**Your personal AI that actually knows you.** Qel is a shared memory between you and AI — it captures what you think, organizes what matters, and helps you act on all of it. Nothing gets lost. Everything gets easier.

---

## Brand Personality

### 1. Agency

The primary brand feeling. When someone thinks of Qel, they should feel **empowered to do things**.

- A student planning their week. A professional organizing their projects. Someone saving inspiration for a trip, a recipe, a dream apartment.
- It's not passive storage. It's a launchpad.
- The feeling: "I can plan this. I can make this happen. I can get this done."
- Qel helps you think, plan, clarify, and then *do* — with AI that knows exactly what you mean

**In practice:** The UI should feel alive, not like an archive. Every piece of knowledge should feel like it's *ready to be used*, not filed away.

### 2. Companionship & Safety

Not privacy (though that matters too) — but the psychological safety of a trusted space.

- The feeling of: "I can dump anything here and it's okay"
- Qel is a place to be vulnerable with half-formed thoughts, messy ideas, raw voice notes
- There's a **reward loop**: dump information → play with it → manipulate it → something valuable emerges
- Users should feel the same relief as talking to a trusted friend who just *gets it*

**In practice:** The experience should never feel judgmental or overwhelming. No empty states that make you feel behind. No rigid structures that make messy thoughts feel wrong. The interface invites you to pour things in and then helps you make sense of them.

---

## Brand Positioning

### For whom?

Everyone. Qel is consumer AI — as wide as possible.

- **Students** — lecture notes, research, study plans, exam prep
- **Professionals** — meeting notes, project ideas, work plans, career goals
- **Creatives** — mood boards, inspiration, vision boards, color palettes
- **Parents** — recipes, school schedules, family plans, memories
- **Anyone with thoughts** — journal entries, voice memos at 2am, that thing you saw and want to remember

If you've ever lost a thought, forgotten an idea, or wished you could just *find that thing you saved somewhere* — Qel is for you.

### What does Qel give them?

**Clarity and action.** Qel knows what you've been thinking about, what you care about, and what you're working toward — so it can actually help you move forward.

---

## Three Core Modalities

Everything a user does with Qel falls into one of three actions:

### Give → Qel (Capture)
Save, share, dump, capture. Put things *into* Qel.
- Text notes, voice memos, images, links, documents
- Stream of consciousness, half-formed thoughts, raw ideas
- "Save to Qel" from anywhere

### Receive ← Qel (Retrieve)
Ask, search, explore, clarify. Get things *from* Qel.
- Natural language search
- Thought clarification ("What's the word for...?")
- AI-powered insights and connections

### Do → with Qel (Act)
Create, generate, brainstorm, execute. Make things *happen* with Qel.
- Draft a message grounded in your knowledge and voice
- Brainstorm ideas using what you've captured
- Generate plans, summaries, outreach — informed by everything you've saved
- Act on knowledge, not just recall it

These three modalities should be the **primary actions** visible at all times — always one tap/click away.

## Brand Voice

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| **Confident** | Qel knows what it is. No hedging, no "try our app maybe." |
| **Warm** | Not corporate. Speaks like a thoughtful friend, not a product. |
| **Direct** | Short sentences. No fluff. Respects the user's time. |
| **Empowering** | Every message should make the user feel more capable, not more dependent. |
