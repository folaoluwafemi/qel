## 2. Defining Agency from First Principles

In standard computing, software is *reactive* (it waits for a command and executes a script). In standard AI, LLMs are *instruction-followers* (they answer prompts generically).

In Qel, **Agency** is defined as:
> **The capacity of an individual to express their intent to effect change in the world and in themselves generatively.**

Breaking down this definition:
* **"Express one's intent"**: Intent is rarely born as a clean, polished PRD. It begins as a messy voice note, a visual mood on Pinterest, a half-baked intuition, or a vague dissatisfaction. Agency means having a partner that can understand latent, unpolished intent without forcing you to structure it first.
* **"Effect change in the world"**: Real-world execution—whether that means vibe-coding an idea into reality with a new frontier model, drafting communications in your exact voice, or architecting a system.
* **"Effect change in oneself"**: Self-evolution, metacognition, and growth. Recognizing recurring behavioral traps, distinguishing acute sleep deprivation from genuine crisis, and internalizing your historical epiphanies.
* **"Generatively"**: Not through rigid templates, but through emergent, contextual, and creative synthesis that opens up new trajectories you hadn't explicitly planned.

### 3.1 The Free Energy Principle (FEP) in Practice
Under the Free Energy Principle, an intelligent system acts to minimize "Surprise" (the difference between its internal model of the world and actual observations).

In Qel:
* **Your Ground Truth is the Prior Model:** Qel absorbs your 2-year voice history, your Pinterest taste, and your values. This forms its prior belief about *who you are, what you care about, and how you think*.
* **Incoming Input is a Perturbation:** When you drop a new voice memo or a link, Qel evaluates how this aligns with or alters your historical trajectory.
* **Epistemic vs. Instrumental Action:**
  * If Qel lacks context, it exercises **Epistemic Curiosity** (asking a probing, high-leverage question or retrieving related memories).
  * If the path of intent is clear, it exercises **Instrumental Action** (proactively synthesizing narratives, scaffolding specs, or organizing your thoughts).
