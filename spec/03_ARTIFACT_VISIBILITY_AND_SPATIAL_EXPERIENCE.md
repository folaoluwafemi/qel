> *"Remember everything, organize nothing. A private, serene visual stream where you can wander through your raw artifacts as they are."*

---

## 1. The Core Philosophy: "Remember Everything, Organize Nothing"

Traditional productivity tools force users to act as digital librarians—creating nested folders, applying manual tags, and managing complex databases. This creates friction, anxiety, and a heavy "filing tax" that kills creative momentum.

Qel adopts the pure **mymind** design philosophy:
* **Zero Manual Filing**: You never create a folder, pick a category, or manually tag an item. You save a thought, voice memo, image, or link in one tap and move on with your life.
* **Pure Privacy & Serenity**: No social feeds, no sharing pressure, no ads, and no visual clutter. It is an intimate, secret space built exclusively for your mind.
* **Raw Artifact Immersion**: Artifacts live in their unconstrained, beautiful, and tactile visual form.

## 2. The Artifact Stream & Deep Retrieval Mechanics

Artifacts are displayed as dynamic, responsive visual cards in an infinite masonry stream:

### 2.1 Artifact Card Typology
* **Voice Journal Cards**: Visual audio waveform, duration, timestamp, pull-quote of the core extracted takeaway, and playable audio scrub.
* **Visual Inspo & Pins**: High-resolution image cards with automatic color palette extraction (dominant hex codes) and full OCR text recognition of any text inside images/screenshots.
* **Web Clippings & Articles**: Automatic content-type recognition (Article, Book, Product, Video) with a distraction-free **Reading Mode** that strips ads, popups, and trackers.
* **Raw Notes & Quotes**: Typographically refined cards formatted for immediate scanning and visual delight.

### 2.2 Deep, Multi-Dimensional Retrieval
Because the system handles all metadata extraction in the background, you search the way human memory actually works:
* **Visual & Color Search**: Search by color tones (e.g. *"warm terracotta"*, *"#FF4D00"*), mood, or visual objects (*"vintage cameras"*, *"brutalist interior"*).
* **OCR & Screenshot Search**: Find any screenshot simply by typing words that appeared inside the image.
* **Natural Language Queries**: Search by half-remembered impressions (*"that podcast where we talked about softlife in Nigeria"*).
* **Serendipity Mode**: Gentle, ambient resurfacing of forgotten thoughts and inspirations from months or years ago to spark serendipitous cross-pollination.

## 3. Smart Spaces: Living Queries, Not Static Folders

In Qel, "Spaces" are not rigid static directories. They are **Smart Spaces**—dynamic, automated collections defined by **living queries**:
* You define an intent or query (e.g., *"Cognitive Architecture Research"*, *"Brutalist UI Aesthetics"*, or *"Summer 2024 Epiphanies"*).
* The Smart Space automatically aggregates all relevant voice memos, pins, web clippings, and notes across your entire archive.
* As new context is captured anywhere, it automatically flows into the matching Smart Space without manual dragging or dropping.
