## 2. Core Feature Capabilities

### 2.1 Multi-Modal Artifact Card Types
1. **Visual Pins & Images**: Rendered in full aspect ratio with an extracted 4-color palette bar and full-text OCR indexing.
2. **Audio Journals**: Waveform player with core epiphany summary.
3. **Web Clippings & Articles**: Rich preview card with clean **Reading Mode** that completely removes popups, cookie banners, and ads.
4. **Raw Notes & Backlog Thoughts**: Clean typographical cards using Geist font.

### 2.2 Smart Spaces (Living Queries)
* **No Manual Drag-and-Drop**: A Smart Space is a dynamic query (e.g. `tag:aesthetic AND color:#FF4D00` or `theme:startup-ideas`).
* New artifacts matching the criteria automatically populate the Space across all devices.

### 2.3 The Serendipity Resurfacing Engine
* Gently surfaces 1–3 forgotten artifacts from past months/years at the top of your stream or in New Tabs.
* Designed to spark subconscious pattern recognition and unexpected creative connections.

### 2.4 Multi-Dimensional Deep Search
* **Search by Color**: Search `warm terracotta`, `slate graphite`, or `#FF4D00`.
* **Search by In-Image Text (OCR)**: Find screenshots by typing the words visible inside them.
* **Search by Concept / Mood**: Semantic similarity queries powered by Cloudflare Vectorize.
