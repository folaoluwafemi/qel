# Qel — Product Vision

## The Problem: Friction in the Cycle

Human progress runs on a repeating loop: *Observe → Reason → Execute → Measure.*

Today, that loop is slower and weaker than it should be:
1.  **Observation is fragmented:** Vital context is lost as scattered notes or trapped in unconstrained, forgotten voice memos.
2.  **Reasoning is bottlenecked:** Our capacity to synthesize these observations is limited by time and working memory. 
3.  **Execution is deferred:** The "Distance to Action" is vast. The sheer energy required to transition from thought to execution (the "Planning Tax") constantly stalls our iterations.

Current AI tools attempt to patch this but suffer from a **Trust Gap**. They lack your personal "Ground Truth"—the history, preferences, and unspoken "Why" behind your choices. Working as generic, isolated operators instead of true extensions of the individual, they cannot seamlessly close the loop.

## The Mission
**To 100x the pace of human innovation with personal AI.**

Qel's mission is to help each person move through the innovation loop—observe, reason, execute, and measure—faster, more accurately, and with less friction.

## Architecture — Four Layers to Agency

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│     LAYER 4: AGENTIC ORCHESTRATION (THE HANDS)         │
│   Sovereign execution via sub-agents (MCP, OpenCLAW)   │
└──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                           │
┌──────────────────────────▼─────────────────────────────┐
│    LAYER 3: GENERATIVE INTERACTION (THE INTERFACE)     │
│   Dynamic UI to clarify intent (Calls, Mural Boards)   │
└──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                           │
┌──────────────────────────▼─────────────────────────────┐
│        LAYER 2: COGNITIVE OS (THE BRAIN)               │
│   Active Inference & the Cognitive Event Graph (CEG)   │
└──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                           │
┌──────────────────────────▼─────────────────────────────┐
│       LAYER 1: UBIQUITOUS INGESTION (THE SENSORS)      │
│   Frictionless capture of intent & ambient exhaust     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Layer 1 — Ubiquitous Ingestion
Frictionless capture of both intentional brain dumps (voice, WhatsApp highlights) and passive digital exhaust (calendar, commits, location). 

### Layer 2 — Cognitive OS
Background rumination. Using a Cognitive Event Graph and Active Inference, Qel models flow, causation, and time. It continually clusters ideas and looks for "lonely islands" of thought or logical contradictions while you sleep or work.

### Layer 3 — Generative Interaction 
The epistemic curiosity layer. Qel dynamically composes the optimal UI (e.g., a simple notification, a VoIP call, or a spatial map) to prompt you, forcing you to articulate unspoken ideas and bridge structural gaps in your reasoning.

### Layer 4 — Agentic Orchestration 
The loop-closer. Once intent is clarified, Qel acts as a Multi-Agent Controller. It compiles briefings aligned with your prior preferences and spawns specialized agents to deal with the real world (scaffolding code, emailing vendors, booking logistics).

## The Three Modalities (The Cycle of Innovation)

Qel's interface and workflow are designed to map directly to the daily innovation cycle, moving far beyond the standard "Request-Response" paradigm:

| Modality | Phase in Cycle | Action & Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Absorb** | **Observation** | **Frictionless Ingestion:** Catching raw, messy thoughts before they vanish (unconstrained voice memos, web highlights) alongside ambient context (digital exhaust, geographic patterns). |
| **Reason** | **Hypothesis** | **Background Synthesis & Coaxing:** Qel ruminates on observations, detects structural gaps, and generates multi-modal interactions (e.g., a proactive VoIP call) to help you connect the dots. |
| **Execute** | **Execution & Measurement** | **Sovereign Orchestration:** Taking the clarified hypothesis and completing complex physical or digital tasks alongside you (e.g., fielding emails, vetting contractors, scaffolding code). |
