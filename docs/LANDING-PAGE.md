# Qel — Landing Page Brief

## Purpose

The landing page is the first impression of Qel. It should communicate the core value proposition in seconds and give visitors two clear actions that mirror the product's two modalities.

---

## Hero Section

### Headline options (pick one)

- **"Your mind, with direction."**
- **"Knowledge meets agency."**
- **"Remember everything. Act on anything."**f

### Subheadline

One sentence that explains what Qel is:

> A personal knowledge base that captures your thoughts from anywhere, organizes them intelligently, and makes them actionable through AI.

### Three Primary CTAs

These mirror the three core modalities of Qel and should be visually prominent:

| CTA | Modality | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Save to Qel** | Give → Qel | Demonstrates the capture experience; could open a demo or sign-up |
| **Ask Qel** | Receive ← Qel | Demonstrates the retrieval/AI experience; could show a live demo |
| **Do with Qel** | Do → with Qel | Demonstrates the action/generation experience; could show AI drafting, brainstorming, or planning |

The three buttons should feel like the *entire product* distilled into three actions.

---

## Page Sections

### 1. Hero
- Headline + subheadline
- Three CTAs: "Save to Qel" / "Ask Qel" / "Do with Qel"
- Background: dark (`#111318`) with accent (`#FF4D00`) highlights
- Possibly a subtle animation showing artifacts flowing in and insights flowing out

### 2. The Problem
- Short, relatable copy about scattered thoughts across apps
- Visual: icons of Notion, Keep, Docs, WhatsApp, Voice Memos — all fragmenting into chaos
- Transition: "What if it all lived in one place?"

### 3. How It Works
Three-step visual:

```
1. CAPTURE          2. UNDERSTAND         3. ACT
Save from           Qel auto-tags,        Search naturally,
anywhere —          categorizes, and      get AI insights,
text, voice,        connects your         and let agents
links, images       knowledge             work for you
```

### 4. The Three Actions (Expanded)
Deeper showcase of the Give/Receive/Do modalities:

**Give → Qel**
- Demo of saving a text note in 3 seconds
- Demo of voice capture
- Demo of browser extension "Save to Qel"
- Emphasis: "From anywhere. In seconds."

**Receive ← Qel**
- Demo of natural language search
- Demo of thought clarification
- Demo of "Ask Qel" returning a meaningful, contextual answer
- Emphasis: "Find things the way you remember them."

**Do → with Qel**
- Demo of capturing a podcast and immediately drafting outreach based on it
- Demo of brainstorming with Qel using your captured knowledge
- Demo of generating a plan or summary grounded in your context
- Emphasis: "Act on what you know."

### 5. Spaces (Teaser)
- Brief visual teaser of brain spaces / mood boards / mind maps
- "Coming soon: Spaces — persistent thinking environments where your knowledge becomes something you can work with."
- This builds excitement for what's next without overpromising

### 6. For Everyone
- Positioning copy: who Qel is for — students, professionals, creatives, parents, anyone with thoughts
- Use cases: vision boards, day planning, study notes, project ideas, recipes, travel plans
- "Your thoughts deserve a home. Qel makes sure nothing gets lost."
- Testimonials or use cases (once available)

### 7. Sign Up / Waitlist
- Email capture
- "Start building your knowledge base"
- Optional: early access badge / invite system to build exclusivity

## Visual Direction

| Element | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Background** | Dark base (`#111318`) — feels like infrastructure |
| **Accent** | Fiery Orange-Red (`#FF4D00`) on CTAs, highlights, and active elements |
| **Secondary** | Soft Coral (`#FF8360`) for hover, subtle warmth |
| **Typography** | Genos for headlines (bold, distinctive), Geist for body (clean, modern) |
| **Tone** | Confident, warm, direct. Not corporate. Speaks to everyone. |
| **Imagery** | Abstract — flowing particles, neural networks, light trails. Not stock photos. |

---

## Responsive Priorities

| Viewport | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Mobile** | Hero + CTAs + Sign up. Minimal scrolling to value. |
| **Desktop** | Full experience with demos, animations, and visual storytelling. |
